## Per-mode reporting and acceptance

Report each checkpoint separately, with all 32 specification rows retained:

- absolute target error in dB: median, Q1/Q3, IQR, maximum from independent measured
  boost; explicitly count undefined errors from no design, guard rejection or missing
  measurement. Do not impute zero or calculate an accuracy percentage.
- verification failure rate: independently failing returned designs / returned designs.
  Also report unavailable verification separately. An unavailable verdict is not a pass.
- optimizer evaluations/spec, measured calls/spec and analyses/spec; partial costs from
  killed native calls are unknown, not zero. Observed timing is secondary only.
- strict success: guard-valid independent measurement AND all ten checks at 1.5 dB.
  Every absent/invalid/unverified candidate fails the all-spec success denominator.
- the new conditional independent-attempt chance reference at each mode/spec's actual
  deployment measure_all budget, with a pool-and-specification bootstrap 95% interval.
  Its assumption is one *already feasible* draw per measurement; it is not an empirical
  random-search result or a permutation test. Keep its name/unit beside every plot.

Use 5,000 specification-cluster bootstrap replicates, retaining all checkpoints and
modes for a sampled specification together. These CIs are conditional on these three
checkpoints; three checkpoints do not establish general training-seed uncertainty.
For each mode, the new target-information claim requires a positive lower one-sided
bootstrap bound at alpha 0.05/6 against **both** controls below (six comparisons in
total). No missing spec clusters or changed input hashes are allowed for the claim.
The bootstrap is an approximate small-sample inference, not an exact test. Report
all six intervals and all per-spec outcomes regardless of direction.

No mode is promoted on median error alone if its denominator shrinks. Compare
precision on paired independently measurable outputs, state both exclusions, and
present full-denominator delivery/verification success alongside. No successful
CI is claimed to prove equivalence or to revise a historical result.

## Task 8: controls and the frozen chance statistic

`hybrid_report.chance_line` draws independent Bernoulli outcomes with per-spec
probability 1-(1-q)^k. The total is a Poisson-binomial parametric null. It does not
permute targets or observations; p >= 0.05 means failure to reject that null, not
equivalence to chance. `final_report.py` is unchanged, including its frozen
k=min(distinct rounded boosts,n_loose_pass) convention. The new report explicitly
uses a differently labelled budget reference; it never recalculates frozen headline
figures under whichever convention looks better.

Paired prospective controls implemented by `mode_error_audit`:

1. **Target-blind**: replace the search target by fixed 8 dB, the declared training
   target-range midpoint. Preserve channel, model, reset index and mode. The actual
   target is withheld from proposal generation, refinement and internal selection.
2. **Target-shuffled**: assign a different specification's target through a seeded
   random cycle (no fixed points), retaining the original channel and reset index.
   Preserve the target multiset. This is a target-information ablation, not a
   permutation p-value: it actually runs the optimizer on the reassigned target.

Each control receives the corresponding informed run's observed deployment-call
ceiling. It may finish early; record actual spend and unused budget. A control
interrupted by that ceiling delivers nothing. The comparison is at matched available
budgets, not necessarily equal realized work. Independently score its returned design
against the original target with no after-the-fact reselection. For `auto`, changing
the search target also changes verification-based escalation: that is intentionally
the whole shipped system's use of target information, not a PPO-only ablation.
Unknown informed cost means the controls are omitted explicitly and the confirmatory
claim is unavailable, not rescued by an imputed cap.

Published claims whose **wording** needs correction, without changing frozen numbers:
`hybrid_report`'s “permutation” / “ON ITS LINE”; REPRODUCE §8 “fully accounted for”
and “More attempts, not better aim”; STATUS §2 “AT its chance line”; inference-modes
§4.3 assertions of equivalence. These are stronger than non-rejection supports.
Their recorded p-values/counts and public retractions remain unchanged. Existing
retractions of superiority are not reversed by criticizing the null. Only fresh
controls can support the new, narrower claim about target information.
